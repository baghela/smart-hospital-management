INSURANCE AND CASHLESS CLAIM PROCEDURE

Eligibility
Cashless treatment is available only with insurers empanelled with the hospital.
The policy must be active on the date of admission for a claim to be considered.
The patient name on the policy must match the hospital registration record exactly.

Pre-Authorisation
For planned admissions, the pre-authorisation request is submitted at least 48 hours in advance.
For emergency admissions, the pre-authorisation request is submitted within 24 hours of admission.
Treatment proceeds while the insurer reviews the pre-authorisation request.

Required Claim Documents
The required documents are the policy card, a photo identity document, the doctor advice note,
the itemised final bill, payment receipts, and the signed discharge summary.
Laboratory and radiology reports relating to the treatment must also be attached.

Claim Status
Claim status is tracked using the claim number issued by the Insurance and TPA Desk.
Typical insurer response time for a pre-authorisation decision is 4 to 24 hours.
Approval or rejection of a claim is decided by the insurer, not by hospital staff and not by any
automated assistant.
