HOSPITAL VISITING HOURS AND GENERAL ACCESS

General Visiting Hours
General ward visiting hours are 11:00 to 13:00 and 17:00 to 19:00 every day.
A maximum of two visitors are allowed per patient at any one time.
Visitors under the age of 12 are not permitted in inpatient wards.

Intensive Care Visiting
ICU visiting is limited to 17:00 to 18:00 daily and to one attendant per patient.
ICU visitors must wear the protective gown provided at the nursing station.

Attendant Passes
One attendant pass is issued per admitted patient at the Admissions Office.
The attendant pass must be produced at the ward entrance on every visit.
Lost attendant passes are replaced at the Admissions Office at no charge.

Outpatient Department Timings
The outpatient department operates from 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Saturday.
The hospital is closed for routine outpatient consultations on public holidays.
Emergency services operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
