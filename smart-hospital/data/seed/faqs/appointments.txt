APPOINTMENT BOOKING AND OUTPATIENT PROCESS

Booking an Appointment
Appointments can be booked at the reception counter, by phone, or through the hospital portal.
Each outpatient consultation slot is 30 minutes long by default.
Patients must provide a valid photo identity document when booking for the first time.

Rescheduling and Cancellation
An appointment can be rescheduled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled time.
Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment may forfeit the consultation fee.
A cancelled slot is released immediately and becomes available to other patients.

Check In and Queue
Patients should check in at the reception counter 15 minutes before the appointment time.
A queue number is issued at check in and is called in order of check in time.
Patients arriving more than 20 minutes late may be asked to wait for the next free slot.

Required Documents for a First Visit
Bring a photo identity document, previous prescriptions, and any earlier test reports.
Insurance patients should also bring the policy card and the insurer authorisation letter.
