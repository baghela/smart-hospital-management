APPROVED LABORATORY PREPARATION INSTRUCTIONS

Fasting Blood Tests
A fasting blood sugar test requires 8 to 10 hours of fasting before sample collection.
Plain water is permitted during the fasting period.
A lipid profile requires 12 hours of fasting before sample collection.

Sample Collection Timings
Routine sample collection is available from 07:00 to 11:00 at the laboratory reception.
Samples for fasting tests are collected only in the morning collection window.
Walk-in sample collection requires a valid laboratory order reference.

Report Availability
Routine biochemistry and haematology reports are available within 24 hours of collection.
Specialised test reports may take up to 72 hours.
Reports are released only to the patient or an authorised attendant on producing identification.

Important Note
These instructions describe preparation and process only.
Interpretation of results and any treatment decision is made by a qualified doctor.
