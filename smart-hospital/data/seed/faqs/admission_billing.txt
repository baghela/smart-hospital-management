ADMISSION, BILLING AND DISCHARGE PROCESS

Admission Process
Planned admissions are processed at the Admissions Office between 08:00 and 20:00.
An admission requires a doctor advice note, a photo identity document, and a signed consent form.
A bed is confirmed only after the Admissions Office issues the admission reference number.

Billing During Stay
An interim bill can be requested at the billing counter at any time during the stay.
Bills are itemised by consultation, bed charges, laboratory tests, pharmacy items, and procedures.
A hospital service tax of 5 percent is applied to the billed subtotal.

Payment Methods
Payments are accepted by cash, debit card, credit card, and digital wallet at the billing counter.
A printed receipt with a receipt number is issued for every payment.
Refunds for unused advance deposits are processed within 7 working days.

Discharge Process
Discharge is initiated only after the treating doctor records and signs the discharge summary.
The discharge summary, final bill, and follow-up advice are handed over at the billing counter.
Vacating the bed is expected within 2 hours of discharge clearance.
